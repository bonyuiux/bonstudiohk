# Website copy: rewrite notes

Rewritten on 2026-11-26 using two sources:
- **Dan Taylor Watt, "How to AI-proof your writing".** Write from lived experience, hold a real opinion, let your personality show. Drop the obvious tells: the em dash and "It's not just X, it's Y".
- **Humanizer (Wikipedia "Signs of AI writing").** No promotional adjectives, slogan lines, lists of three, filler or vague claims. Vary sentence length and use "I".

Every rewrite keeps the facts the site already had (places in the photos, services, clients visible in the work). **Lines marked ⚑ are my guesses about your voice or facts. Please check them.**

## Humanizer check (score out of 50)

| | Directness | Rhythm | Trust | Sounds human | Tightness | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Before | 5 | 4 | 6 | 4 | 6 | **25**: needs revision |
| After | 9 | 8 | 9 | 8 | 8 | **42**: good, room to improve |

What it caught in the old copy:
- **Quotable taglines:** "a soft pause in time", "moments with the people who matter", "something timeless".
- **Em dashes**, in 8 places.
- **Abstract mood words:** "quiet", "honest", "airy", "calm", "timeless".
- **"Natural" used as filler.**
- **Slash lists** where a sentence would do.

To get past 45, the copy needs things only you know: a real shoot, a real client line, the place you always take couples. Swap one of the ⚑ lines for a true story and it will read more like you than anything I can write.
